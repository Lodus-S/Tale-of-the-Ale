**Race:** Human  
**Class:** Fighter

Isolde Seastone serves in the Tsurlagol guard and is the twin sister of Gideon, sharing both his face and a fierce sibling bond. She’s warm, light-hearted, and deeply protective of her brother—even covering for his antics when he’d sneak into her place on duty. Despite her kind nature, Isolde is no pushover; she’s a skilled and disciplined swordswoman with a reputation for clean, efficient combat. Many guards admire her—few can match her blend of grace and grit. She's proof that strength and kindness can walk side by side in a blade’s shadow.
