**Race:** Elemental 
**Class:** Mini Boss

What appeared to be a Earth elemental this Golem was covered in green crystals, some glowing and others not. Hitting or damaging these crystals cause them to shatter and break off however the the glowing ones appeared to have stored magic and upon breaking released the trapped spell.

This was what was causing the Lifetree to weaken and what appeared to be fae magic awakened it making it susceptible to damage.
