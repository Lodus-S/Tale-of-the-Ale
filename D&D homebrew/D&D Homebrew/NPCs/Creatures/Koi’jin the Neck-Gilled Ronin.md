[[Session 14]] this was from the POV of Koi'jin
_As told by Koi’jin the Neck-Gilled Ronin_

The waves whispered my name this morning — _Koi’jin_, the last survivor of the Coral Clan. I could smell destiny on the wind, the scent of strangers and steel. When I saw their ship approach the shore, I knew... my arc was about to begin.

They came stumbling off their vessel — four landwalkers, oblivious to the currents of fate swirling beneath the waves. I hid my men among the broken timbers of the beached merchant ship. _Classic Episode 3 setup,_ I thought. The perfect ambush, the perfect debut for a warrior destined to rise beyond this salty plane.

Then they came. The one called Gideon — stoic, like a rival swordsman who’s secretly my brother in another life. Amsel — sneaky, _definitely_ the support character who steals the spotlight mid-season. James and Jose’ — the chaotic duo, comic relief until episode 10 when one of them dies tragically to make the viewers cry.

We struck fast, blades flashing, fins glimmering! My warriors screamed our clan’s battle cry — “**Nani?!**” — and leapt into glorious combat. But… they fell. One by one. Their blood painted the deck, their bodies sank like filler characters.

Now only I remain. My gills ache. My katana hums with the spirit of every poorly subtitled anime I ever loved. “So this is it,” I whisper, falling to one knee. “My Isekai arc begins here.”

A blade pierces my side — I can taste salt and irony. The world fades. The last thing I see are the adventurers standing over me, victorious. My vision blurs as I look to the heavens and murmur,

> “If I am reborn… may I awaken in another world…  
> with a harem…  
> and better loot drops…”

And as the tide pulls me away, the theme song swells — _“My Gill, My Destiny ~Re:Reborn of the Coral Samurai~”_
