**Race:**  Human
**Class:** Guard

The first line of defence for the city, the Procampur Guard are brave, loyal, and always ready for a fight — though not always the sharpest blades in the armoury. They wear blue tabards bearing the symbol of a portcullis wrapped in chains, representing strength and vigilance. Trained to fear fae magic, each guard carries a piece of cold iron, a trusted charm and weapon against spirits and trickery.
