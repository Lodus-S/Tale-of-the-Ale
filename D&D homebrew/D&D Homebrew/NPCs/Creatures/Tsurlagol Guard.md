**Race:**  Human
**Class:** Guard


Tsurlagol’s guards function more like a disciplined militia than a formal army — small in number but tightly knit and well-coordinated. Their limited ranks make it difficult for outsiders or assassins to slip in unnoticed. They wear red tabards marked with a brass caltrop crossed with pickaxes, a proud emblem of their rugged mining roots and defensive grit.
