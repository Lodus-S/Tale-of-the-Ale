**Race:** Tabaxi
**Class:** Wizard

Morrigan is a sleek, sharp-eyed tabaxi with ink-stained fur and a perpetual scent of old parchment and salt air. A wanderer at heart, he’s rarely seen in Procampur—preferring the wild edges of Faerûn where maps fade into myth. His public works are masterpieces, used by merchants, explorers, and generals alike. But beneath the polished collection lies a secret trove of forbidden charts—maps whispered to lead to ancient ruins, buried vaults, and places where magic itself still breathes. Those who seek such maps find Morrigan… if he wishes to be found.
