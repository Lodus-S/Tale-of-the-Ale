MARGELIN — The Masked Countess

**Domain:** The Prison & The Golden Mirage Casinos

Her entire face and neck are concealed behind a blue-silver mask shaped like a serene noblewoman’s expression. Underneath? Torn apart. Permanently. Rumors say she angered a deity or got hit with a cursed radiance spell. She never confirms.

Her casinos are drenched in gold, illusions, and magically rigged games that only the clever survive. Winning here means serious favour; losing too much might land you in _her_ prison.

She’s cold, calculating, and terrifyingly calm. She and Shin keep each other balanced: his strength, her brains.
