**Session Recap – The Ashen Isle**

Sailing through dark waters, the crew reached a **volcanic island** shrouded in ash and shadow. The air burned their lungs, and a distant **dragon’s roar** echoed through the haze.

After rowing ashore, the party found a **makeshift survivor camp**—now nothing but **bones and broken shelters**. Pushing deeper inland, they came across the stranded wreck of **_The Dragon’s Bust_**, its timbers stripped and cannons oddly **aimed toward the island** itself.

Aboard the wreck, the **captain’s quarters** revealed little beyond the fact that he’d remained on the ship long after the crew abandoned it. In the lower decks, **Gideon** found a **smithing toolkit**, while the others uncovered a **locked metal vault**. When brute force failed, **James** tapped into his Goliath power—growing in size and ripping the vault open.

Inside lay **fine drink, ancient scrolls**, and a **purple crystal** radiating pure dread. Ignoring José’s warnings, James approached and touched the crystal—only to **collapse unconscious** as it vanished.

Turnble tended to him while the others discovered the true treasure: **maps and letters detailing the location of the Ale Coast**—the lost destination they’d been searching for.

Meanwhile, James dreamed. In a **void of black**, he found a **figure sorting glowing orbs**, muttering silent judgments of “yes” and “no.” When James reached out, the figure drew a **great axe tipped with a spearhead** and **pierced him through the chest**. He awoke gasping—alive but scarred, his back marked with **glowing runes** eerily similar to those already etched on **Turnble’s arm**.

Just as they recovered, the sky shook. A **massive copper dragon** descended onto the island in a storm of ash and fury. The crew hid, but when **Gideon** tried to reach out psionically, the dragon unleashed **a blast of fire** on the ship, igniting the battlefield.

The **Dragon’s Bust’s cannons**—once turned toward the island—suddenly made sense. The captain had prepared for this. The party took their positions, **firing chain shots and volleys**. **Turnble’s** precision crippled a wing, and **James’s** cannonball struck true, tearing through the dragon’s chest.

With one final roar, the beast fell. The **ash cleared**, the **fog lifted**, and **rain began to fall**—a cleansing downpour washing the blood and soot away.

They’d done it.  
The **Ale Coast awaited**, and the **dragon lay slain**.
