**Session Recap – Hostage rescue **

After defeating the Gray Maw, the party learned that Kurt, the local islander, had been killed by vampiric creatures. Jose’ confronted Gideon about his strange transformation during combat, but Gideon brushed it off, promising to explain later.

The group sailed to Brush Island, where Jose’ spotted a glint—revealed by Decy the pixie to be a sniper aiming at them. Moving into stealth, the party clicked into perfect teamwork. Jose’ scouted with Decy, while Amsel and Gideon slipped in to take out the sniper.

Gideon disguised himself as the fallen sniper and lured the pirates away, giving Amsel the chance to free the hostages while James grabbed the treasure. Once Gideon returned, the party escaped without alerting the pirates.

The rescued hostages turned out to be Mary and Peach, sailors from a wrecked ship. They explained that while most of their crew had already been saved, they were caught by the pirates while gathering supplies.

With everyone safe aboard, the party set sail again, heading toward the tropical island where their ship awaited.
