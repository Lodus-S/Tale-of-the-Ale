**Session Recap — “The Island of Rest and Roar”**

After reaching the tropical island, the party began their search for the old ship _Mary_ — the very one Mary herself once sailed on. The wreck was half-buried in sand and coral, creaking with the weight of years. Inside, they found more than rot and ruin — they found Fishfolk waiting in ambush.

The battle was fierce but short-lived, ending with the party standing among the fallen scales of their would-be captors. While searching the wreckage, Jose’ discovered the captain’s journal — and tucked within, a dark and foreboding letter that hinted at deeper troubles yet to come.

Outside, the group explored a makeshift camp left behind by survivors. There they uncovered a trail leading deeper into the island, where they met **Slapzy**, a sun-faded and half-mad Tabaxi who was just thrilled to be saved. In gratitude, he offered to serve as the party’s scout.

That night, the adventurers finally allowed themselves some rest. James, Turnble, and Jose’ tried their hand at ranged practice, targeting coconuts hanging from palm trees. Their aim was… questionable — until Gideon decided to _become_ the target. Only then did true accuracy (and chaos) follow, with eldritch blasts lighting up the night sky.

By morning, the crew was ready to sail once more. They left the tranquil shores behind and set course toward their next destination — the island said to hold the **Dragon’s Bust**. After several days at sea, the skies grew dark and volcanic clouds loomed on the horizon. Then, cutting through the thunder and surf, came a distant and unmistakable sound —  
**the roar of a dragon.**
