**Session Recap – “The Dust and the Grey”**

While still at the university, the party split up to follow their own leads.

- **José and James** visited _Quib the Alchemist_, learning how to properly use their vial of dragon spit and trading a few rumors.

- **Amsel** climbed up to _Barbophant’s observation tower_, where he got a lesson in constellations—until Barbophant pointed out something strange: a faint **floating layer of dust** hovering above the city’s second district.

- **Gideon** went to see _Beegas_, who was training mercenaries. Beegas used his magical sight to notice Gideon’s **psionic magic**, warning him not to “get lost in the sauce,” since such power has driven others mad before.

Regrouping, the party visited the **Church**, where _Priestess Martha_ gifted them two bottles of potentially holy water—later re-blessed by Turnble just to be sure.

Then came the infiltration. Choosing the **lower route**, the group moved through the sewers, stealthily eliminating guards upon reaching the factory’s outer entrance. Inside, they were met with a shocking sight—**Mayor Vesper of Procampur** and an **unknown elf**, holding **Janko** hostage. The pair escaped through a secret tunnel, unleashing **Grey Maws** to delay the party.

After a short battle, the party stood victorious. Soon after, they received a message from **Litalis**—his team had also entered the factory, found the **hostages**, and discovered **where the Grey Maws teleport when defeated**.
