
**Session Recap – Procampur Shenanigans**

Gideon wandered into New Port and stumbled on a shady fishing shack where he met a fellow rogue. Turns out there's a local Thieves Guild that shifts its base regularly through the shanty town to stay hidden. They had a good ol’ rogue chat.

Meanwhile, Jose’ snagged a slick red leather pouch at the markets—embroidered with Lathander’s symbol. She’s now got her components all neatly organized in it. Fancy stuff.

James ran back and forth like a man on fire between the party and Battlemaster Rathin, trying to lock in his spot in the big tournament. Lots of questions, lots of pacing.

During a healing talk, Gideon decided to _test_ the party’s skills by stabbing himself. Amsel responded with emotional support and zero magic. Jose’ patched him up in the end.

Then, the party split:

- **Jose’ & James** checked in with the town’s kids about their missing toys. The teddy bear was the last one left, and they tracked it down at the university—Greg had cleaned it up for them.
    
- **Gideon & Amsel** hit the shanty town. Gideon, as _Red Jak_, met Batilda again. She filled him in on the guild’s current hideout and some dark news: guards are taking two people a week... for what, no one knows.
    
    - Amsel, flipping the guild coin like a tourist with a death wish, got grabbed by a local who snatched the coin mid-air and asked if he was _"stupid or just looking for trouble?"_ Amsel, grinning, shot back with, _"I can tell you a story of someone real stupid."_
