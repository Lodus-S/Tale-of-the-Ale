**Session Recap: “The Strangeman’s Shadow”**

Inside the factory, the party chose to chase after Vesper through a hidden tunnel rather than delve deeper inside. Moving quietly, they avoided detection and emerged near the prison cells — passing a sealed door held shut by a shimmering wall of force.

Ignoring it for now, they slipped into the cells where Litalis, Papa Ironfist, Hightower, Isolde, and the Tsurlagol guards were waiting. The kidnapped civilians were there too — along with Captain Grunt, his captured crew, and a man bound in chains.

Papa Ironfist explained the situation, and Amsel quickly freed the civilians and Grunt’s men. Grunt revealed he had been looking to return to the Ale Coast as well only to be captured days later returning — but his intentions seemed genuine, earning the group’s trust.

James then approached the chained prisoner: **Welles Javon**, a man experimented on by Dr. Sliver for fifteen years. Twisted by anger but calm in his speech, Javon offered to help them reach the sealed chamber, claiming he could dispel the wall of force. Despite hesitation, the party freed him. Javon showed the way for Janko and the civilians to escape through the sewers before leading the group back to the barrier.

With a thunderous blast, Javon shattered the wall — the sound echoing through the factory. Beyond the door stood **Vesper**, **Dr. Sliver**, **Varian**, and a tall, featureless **Strangeman** cloaked in shadow.

In a flash, Vesper fled through a portal conjured by the Strangeman. The creature then fixed its gaze on James, Turnble, and Javon — striking with inhuman speed, dragging them all off the upper ledge and down into the lower chamber. Battle erupted.

Gray Maws swarmed, cut down by the party, but the Strangeman proved devastating. It killed **James**, and as his body fell, glowing **runes** burned across the Strangeman’s back — stolen from James himself. Then, with eerie calm, the entity stepped through a portal and vanished. Turnble rushed to revive James, barely bringing him back.

Meanwhile, Litalis and Hightower fought Dr. Sliver and Varian. When the party regrouped, they found **Varian impaled** against the wall by Hightower’s javelin, **Dr. Sliver gone**, and **Hightower poisoned** by the doctor’s toxins.

Seeing Varian dead, **Gideon silently walked out**, leaving the factory without a word. The rest gathered what they could and escaped through the sewers. Emerging by the Arena, they split — Litalis and the Ironfists rushed Hightower to the CoCaC for treatment, while the party went to track down Gideon.
