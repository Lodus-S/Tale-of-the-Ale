**Session Recap: “Gleeful Imports”**

Leaving the sewers, the party went straight for Gideon. Best guess? Newport. Solid instinct.

Once there, everyone split:

**James** sprinted around like a wild dog hollering Gideon’s name.  
**Jose’** went the sensible route, asked a guard—turns out Gideon _had_ been seen the day the party got back, but not since.  
**Amsel** wandered into the big tent now hosting a lively imports fair—packed with drinks, chatter, and folks trying to forget their problems.

Amsel parked himself at the bar between two random lads and started sampling high-end imports. He picked a special Calimshan Hanepoot, shared it with one guy, then bought an entire case of “Logger Larger” and turned the place into a full-on celebration.

Jose’ and James eventually joined. Jose’s Message spell lit up every mind in the tent—one reaction stood out. The “random guy” next to Amsel? Yeah… Gideon in disguise. She kept the act going so he wouldn’t blow his cover.

Kegger and Rathin rolled in too, and before long everyone (except Jose’ and the unflappable halfling Kegger) was completely plastered. Seven hours of chaos-level drinking.

Jose’ went to fetch a solution and headed to the university. Inside, she walked into the entire Guild of the Weave in the middle of a chat with a Tabaxi—Cartographer Morrigan, freshly back from mapping an Underdark entrance outside the city. They geeked out over maps for a bit before Chris the Druid hooked her up with smelling salts.

Jose’ dragged the drunk squad to the Lamplight Inn, where Tallow served the Ironfist Special—mage hand slap included. Sobers ’em right up.

After that? Everyone decided the day was officially done and went to bed.
