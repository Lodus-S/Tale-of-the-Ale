**Session Recap**

After spending the night aboard the ship, the party reconvened and took a rowboat back to Newport. With the factory workers freed and the group having just lost an entire day to festival drinking, tensions were high about whether Vesper might retaliate.

To get clarity, the party went to the CoCaC to speak with [[Litalis Ironfist]]. He reassured them that Vesper isn’t the proactive type—more reactionary—so an immediate counterattack was unlikely. For now, the city was safe. Janko also notified them of the orb he stole from the factory and how if a bomb was attached to it it can be sent to where the Gray Maw keep coming from.

With that, the party split up for the day:

- **Josie** sought out **Morrigan the Cartographer** to learn more about the Underdark entrance he’d discovered outside the city. She gathered detailed information about the location and its significance.
    
- **Gideon** and **Amsel** chose to spend the day resting and relaxing.
    
- **James** trained with **Swordmaster Beegas**, focusing on using his harpoon more effectively and with greater control.
    

Josie continued her investigations by visiting **Beagle the Eagle**. She showed him detailed drawings of the strange runes carved into James’s back. Beagle determined that the runes likely **predate Netherese**, possibly even older than the ancient language spoken by the long-dead Netherreal.

Josie cast **Comprehend Languages**. From the runes, she was able to decipher two words:

**“Torso”**  
**“Barron” (B-A-R-R-O-N)**

What those words mean remains unknown.
