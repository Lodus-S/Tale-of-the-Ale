**Session Recap – "Departure and Dreams"**

The party settled into _Procampur_ life a bit more.  
Gideon met up with his sister **Izzy**, who’s on a security detail and staying at the tavern. He warned her about the Mayor’s shady business and shared everything he knows, especially about the letters.

**James** visited the local **adventurer's guild**, picking up a few jobs for the party while Gideon had his heart-to-heart.

Later, the group finally explored their ship — **The Oceanic Duck** — and Josie charted a possible course toward **three island targets** where they might uncover info from lost ships. Everyone picked bunks and slept onboard.

That night, **strange things stirred**:

- **Amsel** felt more dexterous and light on his feet.
    
- **James**’ copper scales shimmered more than usual, feeling at home at sea.
    
- **Josie** had another **dream warning** from that same shadowy figure.
    
- **Gideon** had a brutal vision: a **mutilated, possibly inhuman woman** whispered, _“You’ve chosen this path—now wield your power.”_
    

Next morning, the party got to business:

- **Gideon** returned the stolen crossbow to the guard... with a prank. The guard cried.
    
- The others gathered more **info on missing ships**, tightening their planned route.
    
- A kid’s missing ball was also recovered (bonus karma).
    

As they were leaving the docks, they overheard **Genna** talking to a **gnome**. She misses someone, and the gnome said he does too... though it’s been **nearly 20 years** since.
