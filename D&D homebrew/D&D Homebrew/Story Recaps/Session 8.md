**Session Recap – Double Teaming**

The session kicked off with Amsel calming down a suspicious resident, explaining why he was flipping the sigil coin there—though he threw in an unrelated tale for good measure. His words smoothed things over.

Amsel and Gideon then tracked down the local Thieves Guild, seeking answers about the guilds’ funding of the expeditions. Instead, they were told to meet the leader at the tavern that night. While there, they were shown a strange ledger full of names of towns that don’t seem to exist—found in a lead-lined safe, hinting at something important. José made careful copies of these clues.

Afterward, the party headed to the arena. Amsel and José spectated as Gideon and James carved their way through round after round, while other adventurers also advanced. Now, Gideon and James stand poised to face Reggis and Took in the next bout.
