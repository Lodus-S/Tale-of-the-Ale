
Brown Leather boots with a conch shell trinket hanging from it.

You gain advantage on checks to swim, and your swimming speed equals your walking speed.

Once per long rest, you can call on the boots to steady yourself: as a reaction to being knocked prone or shoved, you may instead remain standing, bracing as if rooted like barnacles on a ship’s hull.
