The tropical Island, The third island the party visited _and actually where my improvising actually made the session fun_. [[Mary]] and [[Peach]] were part of the crew of this ship that beached and while the crew got rescued they were gathering supplies and missed the rescue. The ship was now taken over by some [[Savage Fishfolk]] and lead by [[Koi’jin the Neck-Gilled Ronin]], _a weeaboo reference that just brought him to life_. After the whole fishfolk fight the party found [[Slapzy]], the last remaining crew member.
