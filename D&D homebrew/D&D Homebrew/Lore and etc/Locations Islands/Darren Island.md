Darren island is just a landmass that's been split in half. a Lighthouse on the one side and a home on the other side connected by a bridge in the middle. This was the first Island the party visited only to discover that the ship that crashed here was carrying [[Gray Maw]]. The party also found the corpse of Kurt the keeper of the lighthouse.
